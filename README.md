# UngalSoththu · உங்கள் சொத்து

**"Ithu Ungal Soththu" — This is YOUR asset.** Public transit and the commons belong to the people who use and pay for them. We examine how they are run — with open data, in the public interest.

**Scope:** urban transit + Chennai first (MTC, CMRL, suburban rail, CUMTA), and all of Tamil Nadu — TNSTC, SETC, and the Vettri Payanam scale-up (12,692 zero-fare buses, ~84.32 lakh women & trans riders/day, ~₹6,000 crore/year). We also watch the commons around transit: Chennai's ₹107.2-crore flood-forecasting system, and the archives that keep public memory alive.

## Desk

UngalSoththu is a desk of [CashlessConsumer](https://cashlessconsumer.in) — the consumer collective. Sharp, data-forward, riders' advocacy: never corporate, never party-political; we praise and criticize agencies on evidence only.
